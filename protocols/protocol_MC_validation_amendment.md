# Numerical validation amendment after six fits — 26 October 2026

All six planned40-epoch models and all24 sets of20-pass predictions completed before this change. The first full validator stopped at its3e−6 absolute comparison of `q.mean(axis=0)` to the saved per-record MC means. No model, prediction, split, metric or hypothesis was changed in response.

The writer serializes `float(q[:, i].mean())` and `float(q[:, i].std())` separately for every column. Float32 reduction order differs from the vectorized axis reduction. Independent diagnosis across all24 arrays found maximum axis32-versus-saved mean difference3.8146972674e−6, but the exact per-column expression differed by only1.7763568394e−15 for means and9.7144514655e−17 for SDs after CSV parsing. Float64 means differed from saved float32 means by at most3.4809112552e−6, and float64 SDs by3.0633420023e−8. Every key/order matched, every pass was finite and every per-record SD was nonzero.

The revised check strictly reproduces the writer's per-column expression to1e−12, and additionally compares its means with float64 reductions under the standard gamma_n float32 summation bound: gamma_n=n*epsilon/(1−n*epsilon), multiplied by mean absolute input. It also requires nonzero float64 SDs. This addresses a validator reduction-order defect rather than relaxing checks to accept unrelated predictions. The original validator and failed log are retained; `mc_moment_rounding_diagnosis.json` records all24 cases. The full source, split, checkpoint and MC integrity checks are rerun before statistical analysis. No scientific output is replaced or selected by this amendment.
